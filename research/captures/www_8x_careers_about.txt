What is 8x?

8x is a human orchestration company. Wherever there is a human in the loop, 8x supplies that person, evaluates the work and pays for it, across marketing, engineering and sales.

Remote-first, built from San Francisco.

How the company is organised

The company is organised into verticals. Each one has a vertical lead whose focus is the value a single person can bring in that specific use case.

Every vertical runs on the same underlying principle. A large number of people are orchestrated and evaluated against one key metric for that vertical: virality potential in marketing, meetings booked in sales.

Each new vertical inherits the architecture and the experience of the ones before it, so every one starts further along than the last.

The aim is billions of people under management on 8x platforms, and a different idea of what work is.

What has been built
8x Social

A managed creator network. More than 250,000 creators across 50 countries make organic video for brands, with no ad spend behind it.

8x Sales

A remote sales floor. People learn to sell on real calls with live coaching, and are paid per booking and per qualified meeting.

8x Business

A B2B creator marketplace. Brands brief vetted LinkedIn creators and follow the work from impressions through to closed deals.

8x Research

AI-moderated user research. Hundreds of one-to-one interviews run in parallel and come back as ranked themes with the quotes behind them.
