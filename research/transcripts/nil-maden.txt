I joined Adex in February as an intern after graduating from University. Four months later I moved to a full-time position and eventually leading a team. That's how it works here. So basically you will have to prove yourself and then after the team will do everything to put you in a better place and then provide you the most ownership that you could ever have on a product. And this will take other companies minimum two years of work experience for you to put you in a place with that much ownership. You're building products end-to-end, understanding the go-to market and then getting your product in front of thousands of people and owning your work end-to-end. It's intense, the hours can be a bit high, but the growth is real. If you're AI needed, if you want to work remote and don't mind the occasional San Francisco trips, you should...
