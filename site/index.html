03The work

One idea,
five times over.

Wherever there's a human in the loop, 8x supplies that person, evaluates the work and pays for it. Each vertical runs a lot of people against one number, like virality in marketing or meetings booked in sales, and starts from everything the last one learned.

8x Social A managed creator network. More than 250,000 creators across 50 countries make organic video for consumer brands, with no ad spend behind it. Creators 8x.social 8x LinkedIn A B2B creator marketplace. Brands brief vetted LinkedIn creators and follow the work from impressions through to closed deals. B2B creators 8x.business 8x Sales A remote sales floor. People learn to sell on real calls with live coaching, and are paid per booking and per qualified meeting. Sellers 8x.sale 8x Research AI-moderated user research. Hundreds of one-to-one interviews run in parallel and come back as ranked themes, with the quotes behind them. Interviewees 8xresearch.com 8x Hiring Evidence-first hiring, at 8x and at partner companies. The system you'd go through to join. Candidates. Hi. 8x.careers

04How we hire

We'd rather see
your work.

8x Hiring is evidence-first, for our partners and for ourselves. This is the whole loop, start to finish.

it's a loop, shaped like an 8.
we couldn't help it.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Your CV and a short set of questions, about fifteen minutes. Leave it half-done and come back whenever.

  2. 02

    The assignment

    The role's practical assignment is issued the moment you submit. You don't wait for a recruiter to write back.

  3. 03

    The evidence

    Real work for the real role, and it counts for more than your past. "We don't necessarily care what you've done before. We don't care where you're from." — Jaka, CEO

  4. 04

    Week one

    Internships start as a two-week trial, so both sides find out fast. Full-time, you get a whole product, "with a market and a deadline." — Hussain
